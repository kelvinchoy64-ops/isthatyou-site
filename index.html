係咪你 IsThatYou

把聲可以複製，部手機複製唔到。
A voice can be cloned. Their phone can't.

點樣運作

  1. 有人扮屋企人打嚟、WhatsApp 或者短訊。
  2. 撳一下，真嘅家人部手機即刻收到「係咪你？」。
  3. 佢撳「唔係我」，你個畫面即刻變紅，全家收到通知。
  4. 冇網絡？叫對方講出全家共用、每 5 分鐘轉一次嘅暗號。

How it works

  1. Someone claims to be family — by call, WhatsApp or text.
  2. One tap sends "Is it you?" to the real family member's phone.
  3. If they tap "Not me", your screen turns red and the whole family is alerted.
  4. No signal? Ask for the family codeword that changes every 5 minutes.
