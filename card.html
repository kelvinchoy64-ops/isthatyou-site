係咪你 · 貼喺雪櫃
未見綠燈，
唔轉錢。 有人自稱屋企人要錢？一定先驗證。

點做

  1. 唔好即刻答應，唔好過錢。
  2. 打開「係咪你」，撳大掣，揀佢個名。
  3. 見到綠色先好信。
  4. 紅色或者冇回覆 = 當係騙徒，收線。

聽到呢啲，一定係騙徒

  • 「我換咗新號碼」「部手機壞咗」
  • 叫你轉發收到嘅驗證碼（SMS 數字）
  • 「唔好話畀其他人知」
  • 「好急，而家就要錢」
  • 叫你過數去另一個人或公司戶口
  • 「唔使驗證」「個 App 有問題」

IsThatYou · for the fridge

no money. Someone says they're family and needs money? Check first.

What to do

  1. Don't agree to anything. Don't send money.
  2. Open IsThatYou, tap the big button, pick their name.
  3. Only trust it when you see green.
  4. Red or no answer = treat it as a scam. Hang up.

If you hear these, it's a scam

  • "I've got a new number" / "My phone is broken"
  • Asks you to forward a code you got by SMS
  • "Don't tell anyone else"
  • "It's urgent, I need money now"
  • Asks you to pay someone else's or a company's account
  • "No need to check" / "That app is broken"
