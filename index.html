係咪你 IsThatYou

盜得到 WhatsApp，偷唔到佢部手機。
They can steal a WhatsApp. Not your family's phone.

點樣運作

  1. 有人用屋企人名義搵你要錢：WhatsApp、電話或者短訊。
  2. 撳一下，真嘅家人部手機即刻收到「係咪你？」，要用指紋／Face ID 先確認到。
  3. 未見綠燈，唔轉錢：佢撳「唔係我」或者冇回覆，畫面即刻變紅，全家收到通知。
  4. WhatsApp 被盜？撳「我被盜號」，全家即刻知道：用你名義要錢一律係騙徒。

How it works

  1. Someone asks for money in a family member's name — on WhatsApp, a call or a text.
  2. One tap sends "Is it you?" to the real person's phone; they confirm with Face ID or a fingerprint.
  3. No green, no money: if they tap "Not me" or don't answer, the screen turns red and the whole family is alerted.
  4. WhatsApp hijacked? Tap "I've been hacked" and the family knows any money request in your name is a scam.
